Three steps to your key

Delta uses PlatoRelay checkpoints to protect their key system. DeltaBypass automates the entire verification flow so you don't have to.

1

Open Delta and hit "Get Key"

Navigate through the Delta app until you reach the checkpoint page.

2

Copy the checkpoint link

Copy the full URL from your browser — it starts with gateway.platoboost.com.

3

Paste and click Solve

Paste the link above and click Solve. Your Delta key will appear in a few seconds.
